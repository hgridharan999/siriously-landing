Siriously. Voice control for the Mac that does what you said.

WATCH WITH SOUND

Say what you want in plain words and Siriously works your apps for you. It runs entirely on your own machine.

Watch the demo View the code on GitHub

Seriously? “Show numbers. Click 14. Click 3.”

Siriously. “Open Slack and say the build is up.”

Apple’s Voice Control goes one click at a time: you name each button or read out its number. Siriously takes the whole sentence and does the steps itself. If a mouse or keyboard is hard for you to use, that is the difference between steering and asking.

  • Works without your hands. Say “Siriously” and keep talking.
  • Runs on your Mac. Nothing you say is uploaded.
  • Works in any app. Not just one it was built for.
  • Shows what it heard. And who said it.
